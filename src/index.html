About Campus Bites

A cafeteria that works for campus life

We are the UCP cafeteria on the ground floor of the Student Activity Center. Our cooks and counter staff serve students and faculty every day with honest portions and fair prices.

🥬

FAQ

Quick answers

Do you accept mobile wallets?

Yes. You can pay in cash or with a mobile wallet at the counter.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Every menu category has vegetarian dishes, clearly marked on the menu page.

Can I place a big order for a society event?

Orders of 10+ items should be placed one hour in advance using the form on the How to Order page.

Are you open on Sundays?

No, we are closed on Sundays. Saturday hours are 9 AM - 4 PM.
