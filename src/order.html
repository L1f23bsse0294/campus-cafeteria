How to order

Five easy steps

From menu to tray in minutes - no app or account needed.

Ordering steps

Order in five steps

  1. Choose your items

    Browse the Menu or pick a combo from the Deals page.

  2. Go to the counter

    Tell the cashier your order or your combo code.

  3. Pay at the counter

    Cash and mobile wallets are both accepted.

  4. Collect your token

    You will receive a printed token with your order number.

  5. Wait for your number

    Pick up your food at the pick-up window when your number is called.

Pick-up rules

Good to know

  • Keep your token safe; orders are not handed out without it.
  • Uncollected orders are held for 15 minutes only.
  • Check your order before leaving the counter.
  • Bulk orders (10+ items) should be placed one hour in advance.

Bulk order request

For society events and meetings. Opens your email app to send the request.

Please enter your name. Use a Pakistani mobile number, e.g. 0300 1234567. Bulk orders start at 10 items. Choose a date (we are closed on Sundays).
FAQ

Ordering questions

Can I order ahead?

Only bulk orders (10+ items) are taken in advance. Everything else is first come, first served at the counter.

What if I lose my token?

Please speak to the cashier right away with your order details. We cannot hand over food without verification.

Can I change my order after paying?

Changes are possible before the kitchen starts - ask at the counter immediately.
